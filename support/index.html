Frequently asked

How do I play?

Open a case and ask the narrator yes/no questions — by voice (hold the microphone button) or by typing. The narrator answers, and the facts you uncover fill the case board until you can solve it.

The microphone isn't working

Make sure you've allowed microphone access in your device settings. Speech is turned into text on your device and your audio is never stored. You can always switch to typing instead.

Subscription and restoring purchases

The first case in each drawer is free. Dead Files Pro unlocks every case, with new cases added every week. You can restore a purchase on a new device from the paywall or from Settings. Manage or cancel your subscription any time in your device's account settings.

My progress

Dead Files has no sign-in: your progress is saved anonymously and tied to your device. If you'd like the data associated with your device deleted, email us at the address above.
