Dead Files — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Dead Files is a mystery game where you ask a narrator yes/no questions to uncover true stories. This policy explains what information the app handles and why. We keep the app privacy-first: you play without creating an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, or any other identifying detail.

Who we are

Dead Files is operated by the app's developer ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at felipe@teaforwomen.com.

Information we handle

  • Anonymous player ID. When you first open the app we create an anonymous account so your game progress can be saved. It is a random identifier; it is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or social account.
  • Game progress. Which cases you have opened, the questions you have asked, the clues you have unlocked, and the facts you have found — stored against your anonymous ID so your archive is there when you return.
  • Questions you ask. The text of a question (typed, or transcribed from your voice on your device) is sent to our server and to our language-model provider so the narrator can judge whether the answer is yes or no. Questions are used only to run the game and are not used to identify you.
  • Subscription status. If you subscribe, the purchase is made through Apple or Google. Our subscriptions provider (RevenueCat) tells us whether your subscription is active, tied to an anonymous subscriber ID. We never receive your card number or payment details.

Your microphone and voice

If you choose to ask questions by voice, speech recognition runs on your device. The app receives only the transcribed text; your audio is not recorded, stored, or sent to our servers. You can deny or revoke the microphone permission at any time in your device settings and keep playing by typing.

Information we do not collect

  • No name, email, phone number, or contacts.
  • No advertising identifiers and no third-party advertising.
  • No location data.
  • No sale of personal data — we do not sell your data to anyone.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Supabase — hosts our database and saves your anonymous progress.
  • Anthropic — its Claude model judges your questions as yes or no.
  • RevenueCat, together with the Apple App Store and Google Play — handle subscriptions and tell us whether yours is active.

These providers may process data on servers in the United States and other countries.

How long we keep it

Your anonymous progress is kept so you can continue playing. Because the app has no sign-in, your data lives with the anonymous ID created on your device. You can ask us to delete the data associated with your ID at any time by emailing felipe@teaforwomen.com; deleting the app also ends the creation of new data.

Children

Dead Files is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will remove it.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect the limited data we handle, including encrypted connections and access controls on our backend. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above and post the new version at this address.

Contact
Questions, requests, or data deletion: felipe@teaforwomen.com
